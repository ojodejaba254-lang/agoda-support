A calmer way to start

Begin with the booking, not the panic.

These four steps help you solve simple changes yourself and make a support conversation much more efficient when you need one.

Good to knowBooking conditions vary by property and rate. The terms shown in your confirmation are the source of truth.
  1. 01

    Locate your confirmation

    Search the email address used for the booking, check spam, or open Trips / Bookings in the official Agoda app or website.

  2. 02

    Read the conditions

    Look for cancellation deadlines, payment timing, change rules and any property-specific instructions before taking action.

  3. 03

    Choose the available action

    Use the change or cancellation option on the booking details page when it is available. Save a screenshot of the result.

  4. 04

    Use the official handoff

    If the option is missing or something does not look right, contact Agoda from the app or type Agoda.com into your browser yourself.

Browse by topic

Where can I find my Agoda booking confirmation?

Look for the confirmation email sent to the address used during booking, or open Trips / Bookings in the Agoda app or website. Check your spam or promotions folder if it is missing.

If you still cannot find it, use an official Agoda help channel and have the traveler name, property and approximate booking date ready.

Can I change the dates or guest name on my booking?

Open the booking in Agoda and review the available change options. What can be changed depends on the property, rate and booking conditions.

If no option is shown, contact official Agoda support from the app or website. Do not send account credentials to anyone offering to change it for you.

How do I cancel an Agoda booking?

Open the booking details and select the cancellation option if it is available. Review the cancellation deadline, fee and refund amount before confirming.

The terms in your confirmation are the terms that apply. If the button is missing or the displayed terms look wrong, use official support before making another booking.

When will my Agoda refund arrive?

Refund timing depends on the booking terms, payment method and bank. Check the cancellation confirmation for the expected amount and any stated timing.

If the expected window has passed, contact official Agoda support first, then your payment provider if needed.

What if I was charged but did not receive a confirmation?

Check your email and Agoda account before trying to book again. A second attempt can create a duplicate authorization. Note the amount, date and payment method, but do not share your full card number.

Use official Agoda support for account-specific investigation. Your bank can explain whether a charge is pending or completed.

Where can I find check-in and property instructions?

Check the confirmation and booking details for arrival time, address, contact instructions and any special check-in steps. Some properties require advance arrival details.

For an urgent arrival issue, contact the property using the details in your official booking and use Agoda support if you cannot reach them.

What should I do if I cannot sign in to Agoda?

Use the password reset option on Agoda’s official website or app and follow the email instructions. Make sure you are using the same email or sign-in method used for the booking.

Never share a password, one-time code or verification link with anyone, including someone claiming to be support.

What information is safe to include when I contact support?

Share only what is needed to locate the booking, such as your booking reference, traveler name, property and dates. Redact screenshots before sending them.

Never include a password, one-time passcode, full card number, CVV, identity document or verification link.
