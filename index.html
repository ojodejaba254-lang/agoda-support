Frequently Asked Questions

You can modify your reservation through your Agoda account. Go to "My Bookings," select the reservation you wish to modify, and follow the instructions. For major changes or if you face any issues, our support team is ready to assist you.

Our cancellation policy varies by property and booking type. Most reservations allow free cancellation up to a certain date. Check your booking confirmation or property details for specific cancellation terms. Contact us if you need clarification.

Payment processing typically takes 1-3 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email with your booking details and receipt once your payment is successfully processed.

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address. You'll receive a secure link to reset your password within minutes.

Refunds depend on your property's cancellation policy and your booking terms. Contact our support team with your booking reference number for detailed information about your refund eligibility.
